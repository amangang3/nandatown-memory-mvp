Two Lanes, One Store

Two AI agents trade. Each writes its own memory — and an outside observer reads the same conversation and keeps its own record of what actually happened. Press play to watch both, side by side, and see what the system catches when they disagree.

Lane A · what the agent chose

The buyer and seller each write to their own memory. Useful, attributable — and incomplete, because an agent only records what it thinks to record.

Lane B · what actually happened

An observer reads the raw log of the conversation and writes its own record, quoting the line that proves each fact. It never sees inside agent memory.

What to watch

The four panels along the bottom: what the rules let in, what got committed, who was allowed to read what, and where the two lanes disagreed.
