What happened
Every step is replayed from a real recorded run
Nothing here is mocked or re-enacted. Both agents are claude-sonnet-5 making their own decisions, and every step, quoted sentence and log line is replayed from a recorded run of the NANDA Town agent sandbox — including the agents' own written notes.
Prices are shown in dollars for readability; the agents trade in cents ($19.95 = 1995 cents). Log lines are quoted exactly as the agents wrote them, including where a long note was cut short.
This is the first demo. The full system — two capture lanes, the write rules, the commit history and the access policy — is in Two Lanes, One Store.
